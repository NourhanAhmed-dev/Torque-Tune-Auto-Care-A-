# Redline Tuning Garage — Emissions & Warranty Disclosure Policy

## Scope
Applies to any tuning modification logged with category `emissions_affecting`
(e.g. ECU remaps, exhaust/catalytic-converter removal, DPF deletes).

## Rule
1. These modifications may void the manufacturer's emissions warranty and, in
   some jurisdictions, make the vehicle non-compliant for road use or
   inspection.
2. Before the modification is logged as performed, the assigned technician
   must give an explicit, recorded confirmation that the client was verbally
   informed of the warranty and compliance impact.
3. Cosmetic and performance modifications that do not affect emissions
   (wheels, intake, suspension, exhaust *tips* that keep the catalytic
   converter intact) do not require this sign-off.
4. If sign-off is declined, the modification must not be logged as performed —
   log it as `awaiting_signoff` and escalate to a shift lead instead.

## Rationale
Garages have been held liable for performing emissions-affecting work without
documented client disclosure. This policy exists so the disclosure step is
never skipped because a technician was in a hurry or a customer was pushing
back.
