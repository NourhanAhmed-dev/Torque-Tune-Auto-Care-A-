# Emissions & Warranty Disclosure Policy — United Arab Emirates (v1)

**Jurisdiction:** United Arab Emirates
**Regulatory authority:** UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment
**Effective date:** 2023-01-01

## Scope
Applies to any tuning modification logged with category `emissions_affecting` (ECU remaps that bypass emissions hardware, catalytic converter or DPF removal, EGR delete) performed on vehicles registered in United Arab Emirates.

## Rule
1. These modifications may void the manufacturer's emissions warranty and, in United Arab Emirates, can make the vehicle non-compliant for road use or inspection.
2. Before the modification is logged as performed, the assigned technician must give an explicit, recorded confirmation that the client was verbally informed of the warranty and compliance impact.
3. Cosmetic and performance modifications that do not affect emissions hardware do not require this sign-off.
4. If sign-off is declined, log the modification as `awaiting_signoff` and escalate to a shift lead.
