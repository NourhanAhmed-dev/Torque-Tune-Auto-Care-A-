# Emissions & Warranty Disclosure Policy — European Union (v1)

**Jurisdiction:** European Union
**Regulatory authority:** EU Whole Vehicle Type-Approval (Regulation 2018/858)
**Effective date:** 2023-01-01

## Scope
Applies to any tuning modification logged with category `emissions_affecting` (ECU remaps that bypass emissions hardware, catalytic converter or DPF removal, EGR delete) performed on vehicles registered in European Union.

## Rule
1. These modifications may void the manufacturer's emissions warranty and, in European Union, can make the vehicle non-compliant for road use or inspection.
2. Before the modification is logged as performed, the assigned technician must give an explicit, recorded confirmation that the client was verbally informed of the warranty and compliance impact.
3. Cosmetic and performance modifications that do not affect emissions hardware do not require this sign-off.
4. If sign-off is declined, log the modification as `awaiting_signoff` and escalate to a shift lead.
