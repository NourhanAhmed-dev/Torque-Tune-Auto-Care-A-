# Emissions & Warranty Disclosure Policy — European Union (v2, supersedes v1)

**Jurisdiction:** European Union
**Regulatory authority:** EU Whole Vehicle Type-Approval (Regulation 2018/858)
**Effective date:** 2025-06-01

## What changed from v1
Escalation timelines are now explicit, and standalone ECU installs (Stage 3) are called out separately from decat/DPF-delete work because they are audited differently under current EU Whole Vehicle Type-Approval (Regulation 2018/858) guidance.

## Rule
1. All `emissions_affecting` work still requires recorded technician sign-off before being logged as complete.
2. Standalone ECU installs (Stage 3) additionally require the shift lead to countersign within 48 hours, even after client sign-off.
3. If sign-off is declined, log as `awaiting_signoff` and escalate to a shift lead **within 24 hours**, not at end of shift.
4. Cosmetic and performance modifications that do not affect emissions hardware remain exempt from sign-off.

## Notice
This version supersedes the 2023 policy for European Union. Any reasoning or client communication based on the 2023 escalation timeline is out of date.
