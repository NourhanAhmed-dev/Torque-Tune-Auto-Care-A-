# Technical Service Bulletin TSB-2023-003

**Engine platform:** FA20
**Category:** fueling
**Effective date:** 2023-07-12

## Issue
Reports of high-pressure fuel pump early wear under sustained boost have been observed across FA20-equipped platforms serviced at Redline over the past service interval.

## Recommended Action
Technicians should inspect the affected component during the next scheduled service and document findings in the tuning log under category `performance` as applicable. Refer to the relevant ECU tuning guide for platform-specific torque and calibration values before returning the vehicle to the client.

## Parts
Use only parts listed in the current parts specification sheet for this platform. Do not substitute equivalents without shift-lead approval.
